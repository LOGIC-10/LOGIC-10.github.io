Qinyu Luo Researcher & builder

I’m Qinyu Luo, currently with ByteDance Seed. I’m interested in language models that can reason, use tools, and work with one another.

My work centers on LLM-driven agents, multi-agent systems, agentic tool learning, and multimodal reasoning. I’m especially interested in what happens when these ideas meet the details of a real task.

My academic background includes research with THUNLP at Tsinghua University and graduate study at Johns Hopkins University. My projects span repository-level documentation, open-source language models, and a wireless stethoscope prototype.
