ACL 2025 / Conference paper

EscapeBench: Towards Advancing Creative Intelligence of Language Model Agents

Room-escape environments for studying creative problem-solving, implicit goals, and unexpected uses of familiar tools.

Cheng Qian, Peixuan Han, Qinyu Luo, Bingxiang He, Xiusi Chen, Yuji Zhang, Hongyi Du, Jiarui Yao, Xiaocheng Yang, Denghui Zhang, Yunzhu Li, Heng Ji

01 / The question

How do agents solve unfamiliar problems when neither the goal nor the right tool use is obvious?

02 / The approach

EscapeBench

EscapeBench places agents in interactive room-escape games. EscapeAgent adds foresight for exploring possible tool uses and reflection for revisiting unresolved tasks, making creative adaptation part of the agent loop.
